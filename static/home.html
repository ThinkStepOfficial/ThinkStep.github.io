About ThinkStep

ThinkStep started with a simple frustration: most AI tools just hand kids the answer, which feels helpful in the moment but doesn't actually teach anything.

So this was built the other way around — an AI tutor that acts like a patient teacher sitting next to you, nudging you toward the answer with small hints and encouraging questions, instead of doing the thinking for you.

It's a personal project built around one idea — no data collection, no ads. Just a quiet, gentle space to actually learn.

  • ✓ Never gives the final answer right away
  • ✓ Simple, age-appropriate explanations
  • ✓ Adjusts to grade level and difficulty
  • ✓ Built-in safety rules for kids
  • ✓ No ads, no data selling
  • ✓ Free to try, no account required to start
